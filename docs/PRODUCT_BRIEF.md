# Product Brief — Barnali Fashion Platform

## 1. Executive Summary
**Barnali Fashion** is a premier clothing and occasion-wear rental boutique based in **Bargarh, Odisha** (Location: In front of LIC Office, near Keshariya Restaurant, Bargarh Road, Bargarh, Odisha 768028, Phone: +91 7978256488).

The business previously operated with a management software reportedly associated with Lexicon Solutions Private Limited. When that software became unavailable, the business owner sought a modern, custom platform capable of managing billing, physical garment stock tracking, reservations, laundry care cycles, master tailor alterations, and day-to-day till operations.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Dual Connected Platform**:
  1. **Internal ERP Operations Portal** for store owner, manager, counter sales, tailor, and laundry handlers.
  2. **Public-Facing Boutique Website** for customers across Bargarh, Sambalpur, Jharsuguda, and Balangir to discover outfits, check rental terms, and submit date availability enquiries.
- **Physical Unit Differentiation**:
  Treats each garment as a distinct asset (e.g. `BF-LH-001`, `BF-LH-002`) with its own size, condition rating, storage rack location, and state machine lifecycle.
- **Strict Financial Integrity**:
  Refundable security deposits are held in a separate escrow liability ledger and are never conflated with earned rental revenue.

## 3. Key Stakeholders & Users
- **Store Owner**: High-level financial reporting, daily closing lock, staff permission management.
- **Store Manager**: Booking approval, handover verification, laundry dispatch, and deposit settlement.
- **Counter Staff**: Customer intake, booking wizard, barcode lookup, invoice & thermal receipt printing.
- **Master Tailor (Salim)**: Custom blouse alterations, waist drawstring adjustments, stone & zari repair.
- **Customers**: Brides, grooms, and festive attendees seeking affordable luxury without the ₹40,000+ retail purchase burden.
